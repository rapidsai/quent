<objective>
Stand up the hand-written, framework-free reconstruction core as a new crate
`integrations/nvtx/analyzer` (D-06) and deliver its walking skeleton: a two-pass
`NvtxModelBuilder` that replays an `Event<NvtxEventEntity>` stream, timestamp-ordered, into an
in-memory `NvtxModel` of plain `NvtxSpan`s — reconstructing RangeStart/RangeEnd pairs by
`range_id` (ANA-04) with tolerance by construction (out-of-order sort, duplicate timestamps,
close-at-trace-end synthetic flag — ANA-05).

This is the thinnest end-to-end slice: events in → tolerant `NvtxModel` out, asserted by test.
Handle resolution, Push/Pop, resources, and stats build on this skeleton in later plans.

**Hard constraint (D-06 / D-08 / Pitfall 3):** the crate depends ONLY on `nvtx-events`,
`nvtx-bridge`, `quent-events`, `quent-time` (+ shared workspace deps). NO `quent-analyzer`,
`quent-model`, `quent-schema`, `RtFsmBuilder`, or `model!`/`fsm!`/`entity!` DSL — define our OWN
plain `NvtxSpan` type.

Purpose: establish the off-legacy reconstruction spine every later slice extends.
Output: a members-only crate that reconstructs Start/End ranges tolerantly.
</objective>
